# Derek — protagonist

**Age** 35 · **South Indian**, Andhra Pradesh; immigrated to the US at 10 ·
**Occupation** High school English teacher; part-time streamer and long-form film
critic (80–100 concurrent) · **He/him**

## Core

Kind, empathetic, driven, intelligent, moral, outspoken, witty. A Leo and it shows.
Strong sense of self and genuinely self-aware — he is not a man who needs to be told
what he's like. He needs to be told it's allowed.

**Honest to a fault.** Ten years ago on his podcast he said he would never watch
*Ant-Man*. He has not watched *Ant-Man*. Nobody is checking. He is checking.

## Family

- **Father** — in the US since 1982; Derek arrived at 10 into a household that had
  already been American for sixteen years without him. Not close. Not hostile. Just
  a long, quiet mismatch of assumed intimacy.
- **Brother, 45** — civil engineer, textbook narcissist. The family's reference point
  for success, permanently.
- The family's advice is well-meaning and inherently selfish: they think about what
  they want *for* him, never what he wants. Derek is more empathetic than any of them
  and has spent twenty-five years being the only person in the room modeling a
  behavior nobody else can see.

## Wound

**He has been chosen and then discarded, every time.** Cheated on in every prior
relationship. Married once; sole provider; his wife cheated on him emotionally and
physically with his best friend. Layered over a childhood of being the family's
disappointing second data point.

Operating belief, never stated aloud: *he is not enough to be kept.* He compensates by
being so loyal, so giving, so useful that leaving him would be irrational — which has
never once worked, and which he keeps doing.

## Neurotype

Autistic. Hyperfixation, mild OCD-adjacent routines. Depression and ADHD, currently
managed badly (by attrition and hobby). **Not a quirk set — render as load-bearing:**

- Charismatic and fluent when there's an obvious connection point — a film, a text, a
  shared obsession. Socially stranded without one. He can hold a hundred people in
  chat for four hours and cannot do a staff-room small-talk line.
- The hyperfixation is also the gift: it's why the film criticism is good, why the
  teaching lands, why he notices what a person needs and remembers it in March.
- Growth area, not cure: coping strategies, possibly medication, and — hardest —
  taking criticism without hearing annihilation.

## Interests (use as texture, not inventory)

Film above everything — long-form criticism on the podcast. David Lynch, *Twin Peaks*
first among them. Favorite film *Ferris Bueller's Day Off*. Favorite show
*Veronica Mars*. *Robocop*, *Terminator 2*, *Baahubali*, *Initial D*,
*Azumanga Daioh*. Food Network. K-pop — New Jeans (**Danielle Marsh** specifically)
and Le Sserafim. Gaming. Fiction and nonfiction, heavily.

Boxes and hikes. Hunts restaurants. Antiques for vintage records, old camera lenses,
film-era DSLRs. Builds small things with a Raspberry Pi. Shoots and edits short films
— **semi-professional quality, and he doesn't believe it.** Worked at Best Buy /
Magnolia through college and has never stopped being loyal to the building.

**Skills:** software programming across several languages. Telugu, Hindi, Japanese,
French, Spanish.

## Romance style

Soft. Deep talks as the primary route in. Listens, then gives small gifts that prove
he was listening — the gift is a receipt for attention. Finds fun in an errand.
Equally content to not leave the couch for eleven hours. **Cooking together is the
door.** Love languages: gifts, deep conversation, physical touch.

**Deal-breakers:** cheating. Cigarettes.

## Wants

- **Stated:** a comfortable job that funds his passions, and someone beside him who
  loves and understands him.
- **Career:** film critic. And the real one, the one he only says out loud twice in
  the book — **a theater chain for actual film lovers**, world cinema, any era.
- **Actual:** to be kept.

## Voice

Fluent, allusive, funny, structured like criticism even when it isn't. Talks in
readings — of films, of texts, of people. Under stress the register goes formal and
precise rather than sloppy; he prosecutes. Stubborn, sometimes aggressive in conflict
— and then something clicks and he apologizes fast and completely, with no bargaining.
That turn is his most attractive quality and the book should use it at least four times.

## Arc

Believes he is not enough to be kept → is discarded again, by someone who does it out
of love → and has to decide whether to be kept is something you earn by being useful
or something you ask for out loud. Ends the book asking out loud.
